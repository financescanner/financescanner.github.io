Frequently asked questions

Is FinanceScanner free to use?

Yes. All market data, the SIP calculator and the learning guides are free, with no sign-up needed. The site is supported by advertising.

Is the market data real time?

Quotes come from public third-party sources and can be delayed by seconds or minutes. When a market is closed, the last closing price is shown. Always verify prices with your broker or the exchange before trading.

Which markets and assets does FinanceScanner cover?

Benchmark indices and large listed companies in India, China, Hong Kong, Japan, Taiwan, South Korea and the US, plus Bitcoin, Ethereum and major currency pairs.

How does the SIP calculator work?

Enter a monthly amount, a period in years and an assumed annual return. The calculator applies monthly compounding to estimate future value. It is an illustration only, not a forecast or guarantee.

Does FinanceScanner give investment advice?

No. Content is for general information and education only. FinanceScanner is not a broker, investment adviser or research analyst.
