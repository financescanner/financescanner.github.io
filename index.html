Who covers what: stock-sdk supplies China A-shares, Hong Kong and US stocks plus the playground. It does not cover Japan, Taiwan, South Korea or India, so those (and global indices) use Yahoo Finance, which browsers can only reach through a public CORS proxy. Bitcoin and Ethereum come from exchange APIs; currencies from Yahoo or daily reference rates. Both stock-sdk and the public proxies are best-effort: if your browser blocks the upstream sites (CORS), deploy a small proxy (for example a Cloudflare Worker) and paste it above. Quotes can be delayed by seconds to minutes and show the last close when a market is shut.
